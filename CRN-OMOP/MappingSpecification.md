# Mapping Specifications

This section contains domain-specific mapping specifications used in the CRN-OMOP ETL process.

## Important note

The target concept IDs documented in these mapping specifications may be non-standard concepts. During ETL processing, OMOP vocabulary relationships (e.g. "Maps to") are used to determine the final standard concepts stored in the database.
